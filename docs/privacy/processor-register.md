## Vendor diligence checklist

For each active provider, record:

1. Legal entity and contractual role (processor, independent controller, or recipient).
2. Signed DPA and current subprocessor list.
3. Exact data fields sent, including SDK/server logs and diagnostic metadata.
4. Primary processing/storage regions and remote-support locations.
5. Transfer mechanism for access outside the EEA (adequacy, SCCs, and transfer assessment as applicable).
6. Default and configured retention, backups, deletion latency and account-termination behavior.
7. Security controls, breach-notification commitment and audit/certification evidence.
8. Mechanism for access/export/deletion requests and proof of completion.
9. Product owner, review date and reassessment trigger.

## Immediate decisions

- Confirm the actual production vendor set; an installed or coded integration is not proof of live processing.
- Obtain the Supabase, Vercel/host, Google, Expo and Resend contractual/configuration records first.
- Identify the web-push and feedback-email delivery providers.
- Disable any optional provider that cannot be documented or justified.
- Maintain this register alongside the data inventory and update it before adding a new SDK, Edge Function destination, analytics tool or email/push provider.
